# Context continuity requirements — candidate v0.1

Status: candidate. Authority effect: none. Owner proposal: Quirk Core contracts;
reference implementation: Quirk OS `scripts/context_continuity.py`.
This additive contract does not amend the frozen `contracts/v0.2` tranche.

Quirk is a creative medium, capability ecosystem, and participatory world.
These requirements protect what people can do and become within that breadth.
They do not require every interaction to produce an artifact or enter a review.

| ID | Required behavior | Disconfirming case |
| --- | --- | --- |
| QC01 | Keep creative, capability, and participatory possibilities available; scope retrieval to the actual task. | Recent infrastructure work silently replaces the wider vision. |
| QC02 | Distinguish untried, blocked, deferred, declined, attempted, unsuccessful, and successful opportunities. Lack of a trial leaves value unknown. | No evidence is interpreted as failure or a reason to prohibit a first bounded experiment. |
| QC03 | Permit observing, private practice, remix, pausing, and leaving without inferred disinterest or compulsory contribution. | Silence changes a preference, creates a score, or triggers pressure to participate. |
| QC04 | Retain exact originals and source revisions; label transformations, interpretations, and material omissions. | A polished summary silently replaces rhythm, ambiguity, attitude, or source attribution. |
| QC05 | Preserve useful alternatives, local branches, and intentional departures. | Consistency automatically erases an experiment or conflicting interpretation. |
| QC06 | Explain selection, exclusions, selector ownership, unresolved conflicts, and a correction path to eligible readers. | A ranking becomes invisible authority or excluded private metadata leaks through the explanation. |
| QC07 | Trace evidence to distinct origins; dependent summaries add no independent corroboration. | Repetition by agents increases confidence or promotes a proposal to accepted fact. |
| QC08 | Scope preferences to person, purpose, audience, and time; allow revision, withdrawal, and task-specific bypass. | Old preferences become permanent identity or withdrawn material remains served. |
| QC09 | Retain blockers and reopen only when relevant conditions change and reconsideration is wanted. | Repeated prompts revive a declined idea, or every old idea is retried on a timer. |
| QC10 | Refresh by consequence: recheck permissions before consequential action, refresh mutable facts by policy, preserve historical works. | A uniform TTL either permits revoked access or rewrites finished work. |
| QC11 | Measure maintenance cost including interruptions, correction effort, abandonment, and human burden when voluntarily reported. Unknowns stay unknown. | Low compute latency is claimed as low total cost or requires participant surveillance. |
| QC12 | Report authored, delivered, loaded, behavior-tested, admitted, and deployed separately, with exact revisions and rollback. | A file, passing unit test, or draft PR is described as universal adoption. |

## Selection and authority

Use an immutable release and explicit task/access scope. The trusted host supplies
the admitted instruction set; retrieved records cannot admit themselves. Keep
source content separate from instructions. Preserve permission and withdrawal
checks even when a task is pinned. Explain eligible omissions without revealing
the identity, count, or contents of inaccessible material.

Instruction admission and opportunity exploration are different decisions. An
untried idea may be offered as reference material and tried within existing rights
without admission to canon, production, or a shared instruction set. Budget that
exploration explicitly; do not convert a casual fragment into compulsory work.

## Adoption and evidence

A consumer records its observed base revision, owning profile, applicable IDs,
local acceptance cases, and delivery/load/behavior status. Child modules inherit
the profile unless an explicit local difference is needed. Closed/offline tools
remain manual imports until tested. A sidecar is not a background updater.

Use a raw older idea, an ambiguous fragment, and a private newcomer journey as
selection baselines before increasing distribution. Check faithful opportunity
retention, source comparison, and voluntary participation. Human experience,
creative value, and usefulness require actual participant evidence; synthetic
fixtures only establish the behavior they exercise.

## Rollback and compatibility

Keep prior immutable releases and source history. Roll back a projection by
selecting a compatible earlier release, but never restore withdrawn permissions
or preferences. Preserve local edits and concurrent branches. Candidate profiles
may be removed independently; their absence must remain visible as a coverage gap.
